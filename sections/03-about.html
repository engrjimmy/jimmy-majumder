About

Engineering the future, one autonomous system at a time.

I'm Jimmy Majumder, a Bangladeshi robotics engineer and researcher currently based in Japan. I completed my undergraduate degree in Mechatronics Engineering at the World University of Bangladesh, earning the Best Thesis Award (2018). In 2020, I was awarded the prestigious MEXT Scholarship by the Government of Japan to pursue graduate research in the Global Advanced Assistive Robotics (GAAR) program at Shibata Laboratory, Kyushu Institute of Technology (Kyutech).

I completed my master's in robotics in 2022, receiving the Excellent Thesis Presentation Award and a special honor from the Kyutech president for outstanding contribution. I now work as a Robotics Engineer at QibiTech Inc., a leading robotics startup in Tokyo, collaborating with organizations such as NEDO, Kawasaki Heavy Industries, and Sonai Corporation.

Beyond engineering, I've spent more than eight years across corporate and voluntary sectors — working with UNDP on SDG youth leadership, supporting NASA/IASC on human-machine interaction and space robotics research, and founding the Bangladesh Advance Robotics Research Center (BARRC) in 2014, which has since grown into a multinational robotics organization.

My work sits at the intersection of AI, machine learning, computer vision, and autonomous systems — with a mission to translate frontier robotics research into practical, deployable technology while mentoring the next generation of engineers.
